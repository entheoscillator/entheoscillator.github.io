Statement

Entheoscillator is three people, a table of oscillators, and a pile of instruments older than recording.

We build long-form ambient music the slow way. A drone gets laid down and left alone to breathe. Harmonium and bowls are tuned against it by ear, not by a chart. Granular synthesis pulls the overtones apart until the room starts ringing back, and then we follow whatever the room is doing. Nothing is in a hurry. A set runs as long as the set needs to run.

The name is the argument. Entheogen — the thing that lets you see it. Oscillator — the thing that makes it audible. We are interested in the place where those two meet: sound as a way in, rather than a thing to stand and watch.

We play for people lying down.

  • Frame drum
  • Kalimba

Everything is tuned to the same drone, in just intonation. The electronics are not accompanying the acoustic instruments and the acoustic instruments are not decorating the electronics. It is one instrument with three people on it.
